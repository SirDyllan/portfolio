Drop photos from your Prevsol Digital / Feltach Contracting internship into
this folder (e.g. office.jpg, project-1.jpg), then set the `gallery` field on
the Prevsol Digital entry in the `experience` array in lib/data.ts:

  gallery: [
    "/images/experience/prevsol/office.jpg",
  ],

A "View Work Samples" button appears on the Experience card automatically.
